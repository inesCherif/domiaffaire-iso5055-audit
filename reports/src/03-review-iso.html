5. Conclusions

  1. ISO 5055 measures structural quality, not everything. This app's worst risks (authorization) are outside its list.
  2. The standard brings precision. Exact CWE IDs, thresholds and a counting rule replace impressions.
  3. Manual counting is not conformant. It is slow and approximate. Specialized tools are needed: next, CAST and SonarQube.
